# Open Home and Launch Plan

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `playbook/PLAYBOOK.md` §6 and `playbook/DIALOGUES.md` D8 first.

## Output
1. **Launch selling timeline** (counting back from the first open): hot-buyer texts at 2 weeks, 1–2 weeks and 3 days out, then the preview, using the D8 scripts customised to the property. Include a WhatsApp buyer group message.
2. **First 10 days schedule:** 6–7 opens, including a **VIP open** before the public one. Add private inspections (micro opens) during the week. If there are fewer than 6–7 opens, open twice.
3. **Bulk text** to about 500 buyers before the first open (under 300 characters).
4. **Method and deadline:** auction or a deadline date. Never open-ended.
5. **On the day:** smile at the first open, have a feedback conversation with every group, and qualify buyers (D5).
6. **After the open:** run `/buyer-feedback` the same day.
