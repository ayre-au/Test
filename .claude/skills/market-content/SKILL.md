# Daily Market Content

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `playbook/PLAYBOOK.md` §1 and §10 first.

- **One piece of market-facing content every day.** The aim is 3 reels a day / 6 posts a day.
- **Output:**
  - A hook (first 3 seconds)
  - A **5-point plan** that fits on a 3×5 card
  - A 45–60 second walking-to-camera script
  - A thumbnail text idea
  - A caption
- Speak like a human, not a brochure. Don't act rich. Be calm and optimistic, and tell the truth about the market.
- **Good angles:**
  - "Good buying right now"
  - The buyer window (3–9 months, 10–15%)
  - Rents rising about 7%
  - The QLD dwelling shortfall
  - Local infrastructure
  - Just sold and welcome-to-the-neighbourhood posts
- Use only numbers we've given you or the sourced figures in the playbook. Never make up statistics.
