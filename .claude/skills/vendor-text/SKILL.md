# Daily Vendor Text

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `playbook/DIALOGUES.md` first.

- **Contact every vendor every day, even when there's no enquiry.** "Update everything, even if no enquiry."
- Keep it to 2–4 sentences and sound like a human. No emojis unless asked. Sign off with the agent's first name.
- Each text covers: what happened today, an honest read, and the next thing that's happening.
- On quiet days, say so plainly and give the action we're taking (bulk text to buyers, call-backs, micro opens).
- Over the campaign, repeat the **right figure** (the market price) gently, so the vendor hears it 3–5 times before any price review.
- Give 2 versions: a **standard** one and a **firmer** one (closer to the price conversation).
