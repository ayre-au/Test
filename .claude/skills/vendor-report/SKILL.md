# Friday Vendor Report

Before you write, read `CLAUDE.md`, `playbook/PLAYBOOK.md` §1 and §3–4, and `playbook/DIALOGUES.md` section A.

## Inputs to ask for (use placeholders for anything missing)
- Address, method (auction, deadline or private treaty), price guide, campaign week, DOM
- This week: portal views/enquiries, open home groups, private inspections, contract requests, offers
- Buyer feedback, word for word if possible
- Competing listings and recent comparable sales
- Previous week's numbers, if available, for the trend

## Report structure
1. **Headline.** One sentence: where we are and the status (🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴).
2. **Market snapshot.** The current phase (from CLAUDE.md) and 2–3 lines on the local market. DOM against the market average.
3. **Activity this week.** A table showing this week, last week and the trend.
4. **Buyer feedback, unfiltered.** Bullet points grouped by theme (price, presentation, location). Anonymise buyers.
5. **What the market is telling us.** An honest read of the evidence. Use "If you can't get groups through, it's price" where it applies.
6. **Recommendation.** Hold, adjust (give the range) or renew (new strategy, new energy, new enquiry, new inspections). For deadline/auction campaigns, mention the reserve-setting timeline.
7. **Next steps.** Dated actions for us and for the vendor. Offer the Tuesday 10–12 Zoom.

Tone: calm advisor, empathy and truth. Under 1 page. Finish with a list of the data still missing.
