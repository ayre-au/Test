# Vendor Review and Price Conversation Prep

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `playbook/PLAYBOOK.md` §3–5 and all of `playbook/DIALOGUES.md` section A first.

## Output
1. **Status:** 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴, with the reason (PLAYBOOK §3.2).
2. **Evidence pack:** campaign-to-date numbers, DOM against the market (2025: 17, 2026: 41, 2027: 70+), competing stock, comparable sales, and feedback themes.
3. **Recommendation** (one of):
   - Hold, with a deadline
   - Adjust (about 10% if buyers don't see value). Give the new range.
   - **Renewal:** new strategy, new energy, new enquiry, new inspections. Consider switching to auction or a deadline.
   - **Take it down** (Lahood script A9)
4. **Conversation plan:**
   - Open with empathy (A1)
   - Then the evidence
   - Then the elevator (A2) or the 3-year decision (A4)
   - Then the recommendation
   - Then silence
   - Include the likely objections and responses (A3, A5, A7)
5. **Auction week variant:** a reserve letter from the auctioneer (Mon), a meeting with the auctioneer dialled in (Mon–Wed), and buyer calls: "I'm setting the reserve tonight."
6. **Timing:** if it's 2026, offer the 4-week campaign or the January plan (A6).

Make it face to face or Zoom where possible. The chance they listen goes up 50%.
