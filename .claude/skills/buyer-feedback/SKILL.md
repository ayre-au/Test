# Post-Inspection Buyer Feedback

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `playbook/PLAYBOOK.md` §3.1 and §6 first.

Rules: **instant and unfiltered.** There's a conversation after every inspection, and it's sent the same day.

## Output
1. **Vendor message** (text-length, under 120 words, ready to send):
   - Number of groups and how many were serious
   - The 2–3 strongest pieces of feedback, word for word, especially on price
   - One line on what it means, and the next step
2. **Feedback log** (table): buyer (anonymised), position (first home buyer, upgrader, investor, downsizer), finance status (re-rated since the last rise?), price opinion, interest level 1–5, follow-up action.
3. **Buyer follow-up list.** Hot buyers first. Use DIALOGUES D5 (qualifying) and D7 (Friday push). Aim to get the hot ones to contract within an hour.
4. **Price signal.** If groups were low or the price feedback lines up, flag it for the next `/vendor-review`.

Never invent attendance or feedback. Ask for it.
