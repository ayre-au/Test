# Listing Presentation Prep

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `playbook/PLAYBOOK.md` §1, §4, §5 and §7, and `playbook/DIALOGUES.md` sections A and B first. `buyer-match.html` is our buyer-match presentation app, so reference it for the buyer profile section.

## Output
1. **Seller profile:** motivation (the 3 out of 5 rule: motivation beats price), timing, and what they believe the price is.
2. **Market story:** the current phase, the 3-year decision, DOM trends, and why auction or a deadline is the circuit breaker.
3. **Price range** with evidence. Plan the early price conversation now; don't leave it until later.
4. **Method and timing:** the 4-week 2026 campaign or the January plan. Remind them that if we're not online in Jan/Feb, they'll be selling in May.
5. **Sell the team** (B2) and **close the door on excuses** (B3).
6. **Likely objections** and the matching dialogue lines.
7. **Close** (B1), word for word.
