# Dialogues to Memorise
**Source:** Tom Panos Black Belt, October 2026. Practise these until they sound natural, and roleplay them weekly (Wednesday is "Going Pro").

**How they're delivered:** in the calm advisor voice, with empathy and truth. Be the calmest person in the room. Stay silent after a hard question, because the first to speak loses.

⭐ = the most powerful lines, so learn these first.
*Lines in italics were built from the notes into a full script. Adjust them to your own voice.*

---

## A. Sellers: the price conversation

### A1. Empathy first ⭐
> **"I know exactly how you feel."**
> **"If I were you, I'd feel exactly the same way."**
> **"The market correction is not your fault, and it's not my fault. It is what it is."**

### A2. The elevator ⭐
> "You got in the lift on the 80th floor. Then it was the 70th, then the 60th, and now we're on the 50th. **Do you want to get out on the 50th, or on the 30th?**"

### A3. "I'm not taking that, I had an offer for more last year" ⭐ (McGrath)
> **Vendor:** "I'm not going to take $4.3M."
> **Agent:** "Why not?"
> **Vendor:** "I got an offer last year for $5M."
> **Agent:** "And I think you should have taken it. But here's the thing: **the gap is no different today than it was 12 months ago.**"
>
> "Let's assume for today that the market is right. Sell for the early 5s, take the equity out, and we're going into a period of bargains and mortgagee sales. In about 2–3 years the market has recovered, and you've bought well."
>
> "If you hold and rent it out, the rent will barely cover the repayments and the tenants will knock the house around. **Whether you sell it or not, it's still worth what the market says it's worth.**"
>
> "We might be back to 6 million, or close to it, in 3 years."

*(Swap in the vendor's own numbers.)*

### A4. The 3-year decision ⭐
> "If you wait, it's going to be less. **This is a 3-year decision.** History says at best 12 months of decline, 12 months of stabilisation, and 12 months to get back to where we are today."

### A5. Why now and not later
> "The risk isn't selling too cheap. **The real risk is chasing the market down for 6 weeks and getting a worse result as the market falls.**"

### A6. The timing plan
> "We have two options. A 4-week campaign to sell in 2026, or here's the plan for January. *And just so you know, March has Easter and Anzac Day. If we're not online in January or February, we'll be selling in May.*"

### A7. No guarantees, but a process
> **"I can't give you any guarantees in this current market."** *What I can give you is a process: a new strategy, new energy, new enquiry and new inspections.*

### A8. Committing to a renewal
> **"If we commit to a new strategy, we commit to a new energy."**

### A9. Take it down (Lahood) ⭐
> "I understand you're looking for $___. I think it's worth $___. **But it's not worth that right now.** We've got the photos and everything we need. Let's pull it down, and **I'll call you when it's worth $___.**"
>
> **Vendor:** "Oh, well, when do you think I'll get my $2M?"
> **Agent:** "I'm not sure, but I'll call you."
>
> *Then go silent. The first to speak loses. Listen for the real motivation: "Well, we need to move to Perth by…"*

### A10. Seven days before auction, setting the reserve
> **To the vendor (with the auctioneer's letter):** "This is the real price that buys this property."
> **To buyers:** **"I'm setting the reserve tonight. I'm seeing the vendor tonight."** *"Where do you need to be to buy it?"*

## B. Sellers: winning the listing

### B1. The close ⭐
> **"How would you feel about us working together? I'd love the opportunity to represent you. I'm 100% confident I'm the agent to get you the best price."**

### B2. Selling the team
> "You're not just getting me. **You're getting the whole team.** We're like a medical team: the nurse, the anaesthetist, the surgeon. **I'm the one putting all the deals together.**"

### B3. Closing the door on excuses
> "We have the most listings and the most buyers. Whether buyers end up buying from us or someone else, **they call us for advice.**"

## C. Expireds and past appraisals

### C1. Expired listing ⭐
> **"What would need to be different for you to consider selling again?"**

### C2. Past appraisal (12–18 months ago)
> *"Hi ___, it's ___ from ___. We appraised your home about [x] months ago, and the market has shifted a lot since then. I wanted to give you an honest update on where it sits today, and I'm doing that for everyone I've appraised for. Have you got 5 minutes this week?"*

## D. Buyers

### D1. The window of opportunity ⭐
> **"Buyers have a 3 to 9 month window to secure a property they love at a 10–15% discount."**
> "Look at the last 5 corrections in the Australian property market. You're buying now, **not competing against 15 people at an auction**, and not bidding over 8 to 10 other buyers. You're getting a house you love."

### D2. Reading the market for them ⭐
> **"You don't know if the market's going to get worse, and neither do I.** Past corrections say we're about where it's going to land right now. It could go another 1–2%."
> "Prices are cheaper than the peak, which is good for you. There's less competition, which is also good for you."
> "History tells us property doubles in about 10 years."

### D3. Permission to act ⭐
> **"If you love the home, I'd encourage you to make a move. If it's not the right one, let's wait until you find it."**
> "You can't track the market month to month. If you don't think it's the right time, sit on the sidelines and wait another 12 months. That's your choice."

### D4. Missing the window
> *"Waiting for the bottom to save 2% could cost you 35% over the coming years. **You'll make 2%, they'll make 35%.**"*

### D5. Qualifying questions
> **"Have you been re-rated since the last rate rise?"**
> **"Are you in a position to exchange contracts today?"**

### D6. At the auction
> **"Why aren't you bidding?"** / **"Why aren't you registering?"**

### D7. Friday buyer push
> **"Get me an offer. We're ready to go. Be there tomorrow."**

### D8. Launch selling sequence ⭐
> **2 weeks out:** "We're getting a home ready that's going to sell. **It's a beauty.**"
> **1–2 weeks out:** "Giving you a heads up. **I can't show it to you yet, but it'll be ready in two weeks.**"
> **3 days out:** "**3 days to go**, just letting you know. Thursday's the day."
> **Preview:** "**This is the preview.** It hits the market Saturday."

### D9. Negotiate alone now, or compete later ⭐
> "Look at the last 5 corrections in the Australian market. On average they've lasted about 12 months, and prices came down about [12%*] from the peak."
> "So if you find a home you love now, the correction is already factored into the price. **I'd rather be the only one negotiating today than one of 10 offers when certainty comes back.** Because when confidence returns, the buyers come back, and so do the prices."
> "You're not trying to pick the bottom. You're trying to buy the right home, at a fair price, without a bidding war."

*\*Check the % before using it publicly. PropTrack's national chart shows smaller peak-to-trough falls, so attribute the figure (e.g. "according to John McGrath") or use the 12-month duration on its own.*

## E. Lines to live by
- **"Price is the marketing strategy."**
- **"Hard conversations are the new core skill."**
- **"The agent has to be the calmest person in the room."**
- **"Negotiation has replaced momentum."**
- **"In the last market it was all about competition. In this market it's all about isolation: isolating the one buyer."**
- **"Be visible when others go quiet."**
- **"The market is separating professionals from participants."**
- **"There's no bell for the bottom of the market, but there is a bell for good buying right now."**
- **"Charisma gets you in the door; character keeps you there."**
- **"You can never do a good deal with a bad person."**
- **"All you need to do to have a good year is have a good day."**
- **"80% of winning is just beginning."**
- **"Learn it all, don't know it all."**
- **"A business will never outgrow its leader."**
- **"Speak like a human being, not a brochure."**
