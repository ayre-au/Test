## 1. Where the market is and our 3-year plan

### 1.1 Sunshine Coast, right now (October 2026)
- **Phase: Transition.** Sellers' expectations are still at the peak, but buyers have already moved. Not much is transacting.
- The transition phase usually lasts about **2 months**. We expect it to end around **December 2026 / January 2027**.
- **What we saw on the Gold Coast:** it kept going until the end of July, slowed in August and *"fell off a cliff"* in September. The Sunshine Coast is following it.
- **The national peak was March 2026.**

### 1.2 The cycle we are planning for
| Phase | Timing (Sunshine Coast) | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Transition** | Now → Dec 2026 / Jan 2027 | Reset vendor expectations. List everything that's priced right. Have the hard conversations now. |
| **Decline** | Jan 2027 → Jan 2028 (~12 months) | Volume, negotiation and isolating one buyer. Carry more stock. Grow market share while others go quiet. |
| **Stabilisation** | 2028 (~12 months) | Investors and upgraders come back. Bargain-hunters and buyers coming off the sidelines. |
| **Recovery** | 2029 (~12 months) | Prices get back to today's levels. The agents with market share win. |

> "12 months of decline at best, 12 months of stabilisation, 12 months to get back to today. **It's a 3-year decision.**"

- **History:** the last 5 corrections lasted an average of **12.2 months** and averaged about **12%**.
- **The other view:** Creagh suggested prices could recover from **Q1 2027**, with rate cuts late 2027. We plan on the conservative 12/12/12 view and treat anything better as upside.
- **Where it sits now:** 10–15% has probably reached the bottom. 5–10% probably has a bit more to go. We are *"closer to the end than the start of the correction."*
- **Once sentiment shifts, the market changes extremely quickly.** There's no bell at the bottom of the market, but there is a bell that says **good buying right now**.

### 1.3 Economic picture (Eleanor Creagh, REA/PropTrack)
**The triple shock:** interest rates, employment, and the Middle East conflict.

**Rates and the economy**
- Household spending is up. The economy is slowing, but is it slowing fast enough to get inflation under control?
- The last quarter's inflation numbers will decide whether there's another rate rise late this year or in Q1 2027. **It's not a done deal.** McGrath expects 1–2 more rises.
- Every rate rise takes **2–3% off borrowing capacity**.
- A negative gearing change would hit about as hard as a 1% rate rise.
- Interest rates are the main driver of this downturn.

**Demand and supply**
- Buyer enquiry is **down 40% nationally**.
- Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth have the sharpest corrections, stock for sale is **up 30%**, and demand is weaker. Brisbane is already down 45% *(as noted; probably enquiry or volume, not price)*.
- Sydney and Melbourne stock is still tight.
- National prices had fallen for **6 months in a row** by September. This is the steepest change in 4 decades.
- **The more expensive the property, the bigger the drop.** The top end is falling hardest and the more affordable segments are holding up better. *"The more something's gone up, the more it's gonna come down."*
- **This is an orderly adjustment, not panic selling.** There is still little forced selling, and new listings aren't flooding the market. Mortgagee sales are starting to creep in and pull down comparable sales.
- Prices are falling but homes aren't getting more affordable. Purchase affordability is at a record low.
- Prices are likely to stay under pressure for the coming months.

**Days on market**
| Year | Average DOM |
|---|---|
| 2025 | 17 days |
| 2026 | 41 days (+2 weeks since April) |
| 2027 (forecast) | 70+ days |
| Auction campaigns | only +7 days → **auction is the days-on-market circuit breaker** |

*Local figures from my notes: 29 / 36 days.*

**Investors**
- Investor activity is **down 65%** and investors are on pause. Their worries are higher prices, resale value, and location constraints.
- They'll come back once prices are low enough and rents are high enough, probably **sometime in 2027**.
- When they come back, they'll target strong dual appeal, city fringe and middle ring, and yield.
- **Rents are rising about 7% a year.** Over the last 5 years renting has cost about $11,000 more on average. Less new rental supply means lower vacancy and higher rents.
- New building isn't keeping up. **QLD has the biggest shortfall of new dwellings in Australia.**

**The long-term tailwind:** from 2032 to 2042 the baby boomer wealth transfer brings a wave of downsizing and estate sales across SEQ.

### 1.4 What this means for us
- **The market is separating professionals from participants.**
- The risk isn't "selling too cheap". **The real risk is chasing the market down for 6 weeks and getting a worse result as the market falls.**
- **The good news:** even in a transitioning market, buyers are still interested in good homes. But some properties online can't get a single buyer through the door, and that's a price signal.
- **What we can and can't control:** we can't control the outer market (rates, the economy). We can control the inner market: activity, conversations, process and energy.

## 2. The 10 rules for this market
1. **Price is the marketing strategy.** Nothing else fixes the wrong price.
2. **Hard conversations are now the core skill.** Have price conversations early, not late when emotions are running high.
3. **Lead, don't chase likeability.** The agent has to be the calmest person in the room.
4. **Negotiation has replaced momentum.** Learn the art of negotiation and practise it.
5. **The last market was about competition. This one is about isolation.** Isolate the one buyer.
6. **Be visible when others go quiet.**
7. **Measure market share, not commission.** The lower your market share, the less you know and the less you can advise people.
8. **Carry more listings.** Volume, fees and time are the levers we can pull while value is under pressure.
9. **Empathy and truth in every conversation.** Never filter the feedback.
10. **Control the controllables.** Great mindset, energy, activity.

**The 4 core skills:** buyer management, vendor management, listing presentation and prospecting.

## 3. Vendor management system *(our biggest lever)*

### 3.1 Communication cadence
| Frequency | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **After every inspection** | Instant feedback call or text | Same day, unfiltered. A conversation after every inspection. |
| **Daily** | Contact with every vendor | Update them **even when there's no enquiry**. Vendors need to hear the right figure *"3, 4, 5 times"* during the campaign. |
| **Every Tuesday, 10–12** | Zoom vendor meetings | One agent said this alone took them from **2 to 7 sales in a month**. |
| **Every Friday** | Written vendor report | Activity, feedback, market, recommendation. Use `/vendor-report`. |
| **Weeks 2, 4, 6 and 8** | Formal vendor review | A price and strategy review. Use `/vendor-review`. |

- Have **multiple conversations a day** with vendors.
- **Don't filter the feedback.** Vendors look for reasons why their property isn't selling, so close the door on excuses.
- Face to face, the chance they actually listen goes up by **50%**. Have the big conversations in person.
- With private treaty we're not face to face with buyers, which is one reason to prefer a deadline or auction.

### 3.2 The listing checklist (red / amber / green)
| Status | Definition | Time we spend |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Green** | Priced well and motivated | **80% of our time** |
| 🟡 **Amber** | Close. Needs a nudge or a deadline. | 20% |
| 🔴 **Red** | Overpriced, 100+ days on market, unresponsive. "Nasty and sackable." | Renew it, reset it or let it go |

**Stop carrying dead stock.** Every red listing costs us time, money and our reputation in the market.

### 3.3 The renewal (relaunching a stale listing)
If we commit to a new strategy, we commit to new energy:
- **New strategy** (price, method, deadline)
- **New energy**
- **New enquiry**
- **New inspections**

If it won't sell at the right price, take it down (the Lahood approach): *"We've got the photos. Let's pull it down and I'll call you when it's worth that."*

### 3.4 The seller timing plan (from today)
- **Option A:** a **4-week campaign to sell in 2026**, settled before Christmas.
- **Option B:** *"Here's the plan for January."* Get it prepared now and launch in Jan/Feb.
- **Warning:** March has Easter and Anzac Day. **If we don't get online in Jan/Feb, we'll be selling in May.**

## 4. Pricing, price conversations and stock control
- **Price is the marketing strategy.** If buyers don't see value at the current level, adjust by about **10%**.
- **Copy writing and pricing approach** (Walk, Avenue Pimpama): take what the vendor wants and price it 10% less. Use a broad, roughly $200k search range.
- **Rule of 3 out of 5:** an overpriced vendor doesn't matter if they're **motivated**. Motivation beats price at the listing stage, as long as the price conversation happens early.
- **Have price conversations early.** Use the 2/4/6/8-week reviews, and use the evidence from enquiry, inspections and days on market.
- **If you can't get groups through, it's the price.** Teach vendors this with virtual inspection and portal (Ignite) data.
- **Find the real motivation.** Stay silent; the first to speak loses. Listen for "well, we need to move to Perth by…".
- **Use the elevator metaphor** (see DIALOGUES.md).

## 5. Auction and deadline campaigns
- **Kollosche sells about 75% of listings by auction.**
- An auction does two things: it creates **competition**, and it sets a **deadline for both the seller and the buyer**.
- In this market, run an **auction or a campaign with a fixed deadline date**. Open-ended private treaty drifts with the market.

### 5.1 Auction week process
| When | Action |
|---|---|
| **7 days out (Monday)** | The auctioneer sends the vendor a **reserve letter** saying the real price that buys the property. |
| **Mon–Wed** | Auction meeting with the vendor. **Dial the auctioneer into the meeting.** |
| **Week before** | Call every interested buyer: *"I'm setting the reserve tonight. I'm seeing the vendor tonight."* |
| **Auction day** | Stand with the buyers. Ask *"Why aren't you bidding? Why aren't you registering?"* |

### 5.2 Escalation
- **Escalation is huge.** Use an auctioneer, and ideally a **second auctioneer as an unbiased third-party negotiator**. This is very hard to do in private treaty.
- Ask buyers directly: *"Are you in a position to exchange contracts today?"* and *"Have you been re-rated since the last rate rise?"*

## 6. Buyer management and open homes

### 6.1 Open home schedule (first 10 days)
- **6–7 opens in the first 10 days.** If there are fewer than 6–7 opens, open properties twice.
- **VIP open** before the public launch.
- **Bulk text 500 buyers** before the first open.
- **Private inspections (micro opens)** every day. On Saturdays we're competing with 60 other opens, so the private inspection market runs every day of the week.
- **Smile at the very first open.** Saturday is game day.

### 6.2 Launch selling (pre-market heat)
Contact hot buyers **2 weeks before** the home opens for inspection:
1. **Two weeks out:** *"We're getting a home ready that's going to sell. It's a beauty."*
2. **One to two weeks out:** *"Giving you a heads up. I can't show it to you yet, but it'll be ready in two weeks."*
3. **Three days out:** *"Three days to go. Thursday's the day."*
4. **Preview:** *"This is the preview. It hits the market Saturday."*

The goal is to get buyers saying, "I'm not going to make an offer on this, because something's coming."

### 6.3 Buyer follow-up
- **Every agent is sharing the same 5 buyers.** Speed wins.
- **Friday buyer work comes first.** *"Get me an offer, we're ready to go. Be there tomorrow."*
- **Get them to contract within an hour** of interest.
- **A WhatsApp buyer group has a direct correlation with success.**
- **Qualify every buyer:** *"Have you been re-rated since the last rate rise?"* and *"Are you in a position to exchange today?"*
- **John McGrath:** you're always **2 conversations away** from getting a property sold. Calling 1,000 buyers sold 5 properties.

### 6.4 What buyers need to hear
- Prices are below the peak, which is good for them.
- There's less competition, which is also good for them.
- **They have a 3–9 month window to secure a property they love at a 10–15% discount**, without bidding against 8–15 other buyers.
- History says property doubles in about 10 years.
- See the buyer scripts in DIALOGUES.md.

## 7. Listing presentation
- **Sell the team.** *"We're like a medical team: the nurse, the anaesthetist, the surgeon. I'm the one putting all the deals together."*
- **Close the door on excuses up front.** We have the most listings and the most buyers. Whether buyers buy from us or someone else, they call us for advice.
- **Tell the 3-year story.** Waiting means a lower price (see DIALOGUES.md).
- **Make no guarantees.** *"I can't give you any guarantees in this market. What I can give you is a process."*
- **Choose the method:** auction or a fixed deadline.
- **Choose the timing:** a 4-week campaign for 2026, or a plan for January.
- **Close:** *"How would you feel about us working together?"* (see DIALOGUES.md).

## 8. Prospecting and pipeline

### 8.1 The job never changes
**Grow the pipeline. Grow the listings. Grow the revenue.**

### 8.2 The 4 ways to drive fees
| Lever | Right now |
|---|---|
| Value | Under pressure |
| **Volume** | Opportunity. Carry more listings. |
| **Fees** | Opportunity. Hold the fee by being worth it. |
| **Time** | Opportunity. Use leverage, systems and AI. |

### 8.3 Prospecting system
- **Set a 10-week market-share / GCI target**, then work backwards to the activity numbers.
- **Must-call list** every day.
- **Call every past appraisal from the last 12–18 months.**
- **EXPIRED LISTINGS (top priority).** Ask: *"What would need to be different for you to consider selling again?"*
- **Protect morning prospecting.** Nothing gets booked into it.
- Match the prospecting method to the market.
- **Go from $1M to $2M:** double the listings. That takes someone getting you through more doors, a prospector or lead generator (a "stunt double").
- **Niche:** consider specialising by asset class, not just by area.
- **Build your network** with morning networking.

## 10. Brand and content
- **Post one piece of market-facing content every day.** The aim is 3 reels a day / 6 posts a day.
- **Video rules:**
  - Film walking. It comes across as more raw and authentic.
  - Keep a **3×5 card** on your phone with your **5-point plan** while shooting.
  - Speak like a human being, not a brochure.
  - Don't act rich in your videos.
  - Get a good hook and a good thumbnail.
- **Brand:** decide what you want from it and work back from that. Build it for business, not ego. Real estate is an attraction business.
- **Benchmarking:** post the URL of someone you want to be like and say, "Here's what I want to be like, but here's where I'm from."
- **Infrastructure content** works: roads, rail, developments.
- **Go hard on socials** while others go quiet.
- **Welcome new neighbours:** post photos of buyers in front of the signboard in local Facebook pages.
- **Post on social media every couple of days at a minimum.**

## 12. Mindset, leadership and health
- **Learn it all, don't know it all.**
- **Accept it:** you're signing on again for this many sales. Accept that you're in real estate. Winners have a winning mindset.
- **Courage.** Have the uncomfortable conversations and get expert at them fast: practise, practise, roleplay.
- **Think positive and optimistic.** Even when you don't feel like doing things, do them anyway.
- **CBT model:** feelings (hardest to change), thoughts, then behaviour (easiest to change). Change your behaviour, and that changes your thoughts, which changes your feelings.
- **80% of winning is just beginning.** Don't worry about the 10th step; worry about the 1st.
- **Do the right thing, not the easy thing.**
- **Leaders:**
  - A business will never outgrow its leader.
  - Delegate. Ask your team *"How would you handle this situation?"*
  - Something unimportant done well doesn't become important. Separate signal from noise, and important from urgent.
- **Energy:** you have a finite amount of energy over a career, so spend it on purpose.
- **Health:** faith, family, friends, eat, sleep, exercise. If you're healthy and the nearest 10 people are healthy, you're set. Power comes from feeling good about yourself.
- **Who you hang around with matters.** Do fun things and get the basics right (Smollen).
- **Perspective:** there are 100 people in a cancer ward who would swap places with you. We won the birth lottery.
- **"Why can't we?"** Bring clients, mates and family on the journey with you (Bell, "Road to 1000").
- **Work ethic, for reference:** Spring Chen works 9:30am to 7–8pm plus half of Sunday (it used to be until 11pm). Ethan works 7 days a week.

### Tom Panos – "My Lessons From 30 Years"
- **Charisma gets you in the door; character keeps you there.**
- Think big, think really, really big, and then double it again.
- Quality friendships, family and your spouse are the only 3 things that really matter.
- Life goes fast. Living like it lasts forever is the biggest trap.
- **You can never do a good deal with a bad person.**
- *Partly legible from the second slide:* how you conduct yourself drives happiness; chasing success won't fill a gap it can't fill; hard work for your life and your family's.

## 13. Client for life (bonus ideas)
**Settlement and after**
- **On settlement day,** welcome them into the community. Be the community-based agent.
- **Book the annual appraisal on the spot.** Come back every year and do an appraisal for them, and put it in the diary then and there.
- Keep them up to date with a newsletter, videos and market updates.
- **Welcome neighbours:** post photos in front of the sign in local Facebook groups.

**VIP night (before 5 December)**
- Invite:
  - 10 past vendors who haven't signed yet
  - Current vendors
  - Past vendors who would take a bullet for you
  - Some of your best buyers
- Gifts, drinks, and a face-to-face talk about where the market is.

**Gifts and touches**
- Christmas pudding, umbrella, t-shirts, candles.
- **Car wash deal:** 5 free car washes, one a month. The TV at the car wash shows our listings.
- **First calendar out for the year:** it's the one that gets put up on the fridge.

**Community and lead gen**
- **Charity instant estimate:** "Scan this instant estimate and we'll donate $100 to charity per scan and entry."
- Local events, council involvement, staying up to date with sales.
- **Off-market board:** $400 for an off-market sale board that stays up until settlement.
- Ray White works in patches (geographic farming).

## 14. Team, AI and systems
- **Daily market tracker (Walk, Avenue Pimpama):** use the Firecrawl plugin to log what's listed, sold, under contract and withdrawn every day. This is the "know your stock" discipline.
- **Copy is written with a Claude skill.** See the `.claude/skills` folder in this repo.

- *"No one using AI is super successful… yet."* The opportunity is to be first.

- **Recruit now.** Good agents are getting beaten up by their principals.
- **Bell:** work 2 out of 3 with the "big dogs" (they have the worst processes). Wingman X.
- **Clear lanes in the team.**
